General Failure RulesFor every failure case:\- failure must be controlled;\- public responses must not expose internal exception details;\- no successful itinerary may be fabricated;\- no partial state may be committed;\- version must remain unchanged unless the contract explicitly defines otherwise;\- stale/concurrent failures must not overwrite newer state;\- hidden provider or FastAPI fallback must not occur unless explicitly approved;\- evidence must be tied to the exact tested SHA/environment;\- raw credentials, tokens and secrets must never be stored.\---\# 2.
